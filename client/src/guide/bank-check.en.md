# Bank check

For the accountant. It shows the money that came into and went out of each account, line by line, so you can tick it off against the bank statement.

## Open it

**Accounting → Bank check.** Choose the dates (last 7 days, this month, last month, or your own dates, up to 62 days) and, if you like, one account.

## What you see

- **By account**: money in, money out, bank fees and net for each account of the books (bank, cash, QRIS settlement and so on). A payment method goes to the account chosen for it in **Accounting → Accounts**, so EDC and QRIS of one bank sit together, as on its statement.
- **Each account**: every line with the date, what it was (room balance, extras, group payment, refund, expense, supplier payment, vendor payout), method, room / guest / supplier, reference, and the amounts. Each day has a total. A line opens its reservation, group or event.

## Money in

The same lines as **Cashier Closing**, so the total equals "money received" on Daily Close. The line under the table checks this for you. Stays billed to an agent are not money and are not listed; when the agent or OTA pays, that payment is.

## Money out

Expenses, supplier payments and vendor payouts, on the day they were paid, shown in red.

## Gross, fee and net

- **Gross**: what the guest paid.
- **Fee**: the bank's share. The owner sets it per method in **Settings → Sources & Payments → Edit** (Bank fee %).
- **Net**: gross less fee, what the bank pays out.
- **Bank pays**: the expected day. Set **Bank pays after (days)** per method (QRIS and card: 1). Saturday and Sunday move to Monday. It is an estimate; a holiday is not known.

Fee and days are only used here. They do not change the books or any other report.

## Excel

**Excel** gives a Summary sheet and one sheet per account, with day totals, a filter and an empty **On statement ✓** column to tick by hand.

## Good to know

- A line with **No account chosen** needs an account for its payment method in Accounting → Accounts.
- Agoda and other OTA payouts that cover many bookings are one line at the bank. Use the reference of the payout to match them.
- Cash is listed too: check it against the Cashier Closing instead of a bank statement.
