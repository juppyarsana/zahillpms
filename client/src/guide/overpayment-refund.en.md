# Guest paid too much — refund it

A guest transferred **more than the bill** (the hotel billed the wrong amount, or the guest rounded up). The right answer is to **give the extra back** so the books show what the hotel really kept. **Never add a charge to the folio just to make the balance zero** — that invents revenue the hotel did not earn.

> Example: the bill is Rp 22.500.000, the guest transferred Rp 22.775.000. Refund **Rp 275.000**. Do not add a "Rp 275.000" line.

## 1. Check what was received and what the bill is

- **One booking:** Folio tab → *Estimated Balance Due* and *Payments received*.
- **A group billed as one bill:** group page → **Group Bill** card (bill, paid, credit).

The difference is the **credit** — the amount to refund. If the balance is not a credit, something else is wrong (a wrong line, not an overpayment): fix the bill first.

## 2. If a charge was added to cover the difference, take it off first

If someone already added a line to make the balance zero (an item such as "Other charge" or "Refund"):

1. Folio tab → void that line (a reason is required), **or** Sales → History → **Void** on that sale (reason required). Voiding the sale also voids its folio line.
2. The bill goes back to the correct amount and a **credit** appears.

## 3. Refund the credit

- **One booking, or a group paid room by room:** booking page → ⋮ → **Refund**.
- **A group billed as one bill:** group page → **💸 Refund** on the Group Bill card.

Fill in:
- **Amount** — never more than the credit.
- **Method** — the way the money really goes back: *Bank transfer* if you transfer it, *Cash* if it comes from the drawer.
- **Date** — the day the money is actually given back.
- **Reference** — the transfer reference (or who received the cash).
- **Reason** — for example "guest transferred Rp 22.775.000 for a Rp 22.500.000 bill".

> You need the **Corrections** permission (or be the owner) to void and refund.

## 4. Check

- The balance is **0** and the credit is gone.
- **Cashier Closing** and the **Daily Close** of the refund day show a **minus** line for the refund, under the method you chose.
- The original payment stays on its own day. The books net the two, so finance sees Rp 22.500.000 kept.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Don't** use a Sales item as a "refund" — it adds a positive charge and counts as revenue.
- **Don't** edit the received payment's amount. Undo it and record it again, or refund the difference.
- For a refund given in **cash**, the drawer must really have the cash: the closing shows it as money going out.
