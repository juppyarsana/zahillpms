# Vendor payouts — tours & transport

Some tours and transfers (jeep tracking, a transfer to Amed…) are run by an outside **vendor**. The guest always pays the hotel; the hotel pays the vendor their part afterwards. **[Finance → Vendor payouts](/vendor-payouts)** shows what each trip owes each vendor and records the money paid.

You need the **Vendor prices & payouts** permission (owners always have it). Staff without it only see the guest's price, never the vendor's.

## 1. Set up the activity (once)

1. **Activities → Catalog → Edit** the activity.
2. Under **Vendor**, choose the vendor (or type a name and press **Add new vendor**).
3. How the vendor is paid: **per trip**, **per person**, or **typed for each trip** (when it changes every time).
4. **The vendor's part in the books**: *Taken off revenue (rebate)* — the usual choice — or *Booked as a cost*. Ask your accountant.
5. Tax: choose **Included in the price (all-in)**, then:
   - **Service charge** — *None on this activity* when the hotel charges no service on it.
   - **Tax worked out on** — *Hotel's part only*: tax only on what the hotel keeps (the price less the vendor's part), not on the whole price.
6. **Save**. This applies to bookings made from now on.

An activity can have more than one vendor (e.g. jeep + guide).

## 2. Booking the trip

Book the activity as usual (Activities → New Booking, or the reservation's Activities tab). Each vendor's amount for that trip is filled in automatically. Nothing changes for the guest.

A trip is owed to the vendor once it is **Confirmed** or **Completed**. When it is **cancelled** or a **no-show**, the vendor is owed nothing, unless you tick *the vendor is still owed this amount (a fee)* on the trip.

## 3. Prices to set

When the vendor's price is *typed for each trip*, the trip shows **to set** (Vendors tab: "1 price to set"; Trips tab: **Price to set**). Open the trip with **Edit**, type the amount and **Save**.

On **Edit** you can also change one trip's price (e.g. after bargaining), add a note, add another vendor to that trip, or take a vendor off a trip that has not been paid.

## 4. Paying a vendor

1. **Vendors** tab → **Pay** on the vendor.
2. Trips already done are ticked; tick upcoming ones too if you pay in advance.
3. Paying only part? Type it in **Amount paid**: it is spread over the ticked trips, oldest first. You can also change **Pay now** on each trip.
4. **Paid with** (usually Cash), **Date paid**, a reference if any → **Pay**.
5. **Print slip** — the vendor signs it as their receipt.

This is the accountant's money: it is not in the front office cash drawer or Cashier Closing.

**Paid by mistake?** **Payouts** tab → **Void** with a reason. The payout stays on the list, struck through, and its trips are owed again.

## Where it shows

- **Reports → Revenue**: each activity shows the vendor's part and what the hotel keeps.
- **Accounting**: on the trip's day the vendor's part is taken off revenue (or booked as a cost) and shown as owed to the vendor; a payout clears it. Nothing to enter by hand.
