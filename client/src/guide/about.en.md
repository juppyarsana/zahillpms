# About this guide

This guide explains how to use the PMS for everyday work. Each chapter covers one part of the PMS. It is updated together with the PMS: when a feature is added or a way of working changes, its chapter changes too.

## How to use it

- Pick a chapter from the list on the left (on a phone: the picker at the top).
- Switch language with the **ID / EN** buttons at the top right.
- Press **🖨 Print** to print the chapter you are reading.
- Blue words inside a chapter are links: click to open that page of the PMS.

## Something unclear?

If a step doesn't match what you see in the PMS, or a task you do isn't in the guide yet, tell the owner. The guide will be corrected.

> This guide is for hotel staff. Don't share it with guests or agents.
