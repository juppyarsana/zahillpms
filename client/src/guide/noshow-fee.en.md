# No-show & late-cancellation fees

When a guest does not come (a no-show) or cancels too close to the stay, the hotel is normally entitled to a **fee** — payment for a room it held and could not resell. For an OTA booking the guest already paid to the OTA (for example Agoda), **the OTA pays this fee to the hotel**, usually the **first night**, as long as the no-show was reported to the OTA in time.

> The fee is **its own revenue line** ("No-show & cancellation fees"). It is **not** a room night sold, so occupancy and ADR do not change.

## When marking a no-show or cancelling

1. Open the booking → ⋮ → **Mark No-Show** (or **Cancel Booking**).
2. In the window, choose the fee:
   - **No fee** — nothing is charged (the default, unless an OTA collected the money).
   - **First night** — the first night's price, incl. service & tax.
   - **Whole stay** — the whole booking.
   - **Another amount** — type the amount.
3. Confirm. The room is free again and the fee goes onto the folio.

> A booking from an OTA that **collected the payment**: the default is First night. Check the OTA's extranet for what it pays and use **Another amount** if it differs.
> A booking the guest **pays at the hotel**: charge a fee only if the hotel can really collect it (a card on file, a deposit). With nothing to collect, choose **No fee**.

## A booking already marked no-show or cancelled

Open the booking → ⋮ → **Add no-show fee** (or **Add late-cancellation fee**). Choose the fee, give a reason, save. The fee is dated the **arrival day** (no-show) or the **day it was cancelled**.

## Taking the payment

**Booked through an OTA that collected the money (e.g. Agoda), or billed to an agent:** the fee is **not the guest's to pay**. It goes by itself onto that OTA's / agent's **account in Receivables** (the booking shows *On Agoda's account*), and you record it with the OTA's payout like any other stay: **OTA payouts → Record payout**, tick the booking, enter the amount that reached the bank (the commission kept back fills itself in). Don't use Record Payment for it.

**Paid at the hotel (direct, or an OTA booking the guest pays at the hotel):** **Folio** tab → **Record Payment** → tick the fee line → choose the method → save. Money **already received** on that booking counts; if it is more than the fee, give the difference back with **Refund**.

## Wrong? Take the fee off

- Folio → void the fee line (a reason is required). Then another fee can be added.
- **Undo No-Show** or **Reinstate Booking** takes the fee off by itself. A payment already taken for the fee stays on the booking as a credit — give it back with Refund if needed.

## In the reports and the books

- Reports: a **No-show & cancellation fees** line in Revenue, the Excel, the PDF, Daily Close, Weekly and Monthly.
- Journal: Dr Guest ledger / Cr **No-show & Cancellation Revenue (400-11-002)** + service + tax, on the day the fee counts. On the P&L it shows under **Rooms**.
- Not available for a group billed as one bill or a complimentary stay.
