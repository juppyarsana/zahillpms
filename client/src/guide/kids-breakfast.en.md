# Children & free breakfast

A reservation can record **children** next to the adults. When the hotel covers the children's breakfast on that reservation, FO ticks **Kids eat breakfast free**. The kitchen and the restaurant (POS) see it straight away.

> Children **don't change the price**, the meal plan or the folio. A free kids' breakfast is **not revenue**.

## How to enter it

- **New reservation:** on the room card fill in **Adults** and **Children**. With more than 0 children, the tick **Kids eat breakfast free — the hotel covers it** appears.
- **Existing reservation:** **⋮ → Edit Details** → **Children** and the same tick.

The tick only counts when the room's meal plan **includes breakfast** (BB, HB, FB). Every change is in **Edit History**.

## Where it shows

| Place | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Booking page** | Guests "2 + 1 child", marked "breakfast free" |
| **Guest Lists** | "+ 1 child · breakfast free" under the guest count; breakfast summary "24 pax + 1 kid" |
| **Kitchen** (screen and PDF) | "+ 1 kid · free" per room, total "24 pax + 1 kid" |
| **Forecast** | Breakfast column "+1 kid" |
| **Registration Card** | No. of Guests "2 + 1 child" |
| **POS → Breakfast** | "🧒 + 1 kid · free (hotel)" on that room. When pressing **Came**, the free **Kids meal** is already filled in |

Children whose breakfast is **not** covered show in the POS as "breakfast not included". If such a child eats, the restaurant makes a normal order (paid or Charge to Room).
