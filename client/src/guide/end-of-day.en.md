# End of day

**Front Desk → End of day** has two tabs: **Cashier closing** (the money taken) and **Night audit** (the day closed).

## The order

1. **End of each shift** — **Cashier closing**: pick the day and **Payments taken by** = you. Tick each line against the cash drawer, the EDC slips and the transfers. **Download PDF**, sign it and hand it over.
2. **Before going home** — on **Today**, check out everyone leaving and sort out arrivals who didn't come (check them in, or mark no-show / cancel). Whatever is left the night audit marks no-show.
3. **03:00 by itself** — the night audit posts the night's room charges and closes the day. Next morning open **Night audit** and tap the day for its report (and its PDF).

Only the owner can run the audit by hand (**Run audit now**).

Old links to Cashier Closing or Night Audit open the matching tab here.
