# Housekeeping

**[Operations → Housekeeping](/housekeeping)** shows every room's state: to clean, ready, guest in room, do not disturb, or out of order. It updates by itself, so it can stay open on a phone or computer. You need the **Housekeeping** permission (owners always have it).

## What the colours mean

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Yellow — **To clean** | The guest checked out; clean the room |
| Orange — **Clean on request** | The guest is still in the room and asked for a clean |
| Green — **Ready** | Clean and empty |
| Grey — **Guest in room** | A guest is there, nothing to do |
| Purple — **Do not disturb** | Don't knock |
| Dark grey — **Out of order** | Broken, not for sale |

The **ARRIVAL** tag = a guest arrives in that room today.

## Which room first

The **Clean these first** box at the top lists rooms to clean **with** a guest arriving today, the longest waiting first. Start at number 1. Below it (**After these**) are the other rooms to clean.

Tap a state at the top (e.g. **To clean**) to see only those rooms.

## Marking a room clean

- On the **room tablet**: **Mark Room Clean** (as before).
- On this page: tap the room → **Mark clean** → **Yes, it's clean**.

Front desk gets a Telegram message "Room clean" straight away, with the guest arriving today who can now check in.

## A guest asks for their room to be cleaned

- The guest presses **Clean Room** on the room tablet, **or**
- The guest phones the front desk: front desk opens the room on the Dashboard (or on this page) → **Clean on request**.

Housekeeping gets a Telegram message saying **who asked** (the guest on the tablet, or front desk with their name). Once cleaned, mark it clean as usual. Pressed by mistake: **Cancel the request**.

## Telegram messages

| Message | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Room to clean | Housekeeping | A guest checks out ("clean first" when a guest arrives today) |
| Clean on request | Housekeeping | The guest or front desk asks for a clean |
| Do not disturb | Housekeeping (and front desk) | The guest turns on Do Not Disturb |
| Room clean | Front desk | A room is marked clean |

The owner chooses who gets what in **[Settings → Notifications](/settings/reports)**: tick **Housekeeping — rooms to clean** for the housekeeping group and **Room clean** for front desk. Each recipient has a message language (**English** or **Bahasa Indonesia**).

> Other tasks (repairs, supplies) are still on the old task list — link at the bottom of the Housekeeping page.
