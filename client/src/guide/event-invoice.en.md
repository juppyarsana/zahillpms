# One invoice for rooms + a meeting

A meeting or event (Events menu) has **its own bill**. When the guest or company wants **one invoice with everything** (rooms and the meeting), link the event to their stay and print the combined invoice.

> Only the **printed invoice** is combined. The rooms and the event still each have their own bill and their own payments. Reports and accounting do not change.

## 1. Link the event to the stay

1. Open the event → **⋮ More** → **Edit details**.
2. Under **Rooms for this event**, search for the guest's or the group booker's name.
   - A **group** (👥): pick the group, not one of its rooms.
   - A **single stay** (🛏): pick the reservation.
3. Press **Save changes**.

An event is linked to **one** group or **one** stay. Several guests who booked separately? Put them in one group first (booking **⋮ → Move into a Group**), then link the group.

## 2. Print the combined invoice

From any of these places → **Documents**:

| Page | Menu item |
| --- | --- |
| The event | **Invoice — rooms + event** / **Pro Forma — rooms + event** |
| The booking | **Invoice — room + event** / **Pro Forma — room + event** |
| The group | **Invoice — rooms + event** / **Pro Forma — rooms + event** |

The invoice shows the **rooms** (as on their own invoice), then **each linked event**, then a **summary**: total, paid and balance for the rooms and for each event, and one **Balance due**.

- **Invoice** = what is posted so far. Room nights are posted at the night audit, so before arrival the rooms show Rp 0. Use the **Pro Forma** for an estimate of the whole stay.
- A cancelled event is left out.

## 3. Taking the payment

Use the usual **Record Payment** on the booking (or **Record Group Payment** on the group). When a linked meeting still owes money, the window has one more part at the bottom: **Meeting / event**.

1. Tick the room lines as usual.
2. Tick the meeting and check **Pay now** (type less for a part payment).
3. Choose the method, date and reference once, then **Receive**.

The PMS records the rooms' part on the booking and the meeting's part on the event, with the same method, date and reference. The meeting part starts unticked: leave it unticked when the company pays the meeting on its own.

The event page's own **Record payment** still works for a meeting paid on its own.

A client who wants **separate invoices** keeps using the event's own **Invoice** and the room's own invoice, as before.
