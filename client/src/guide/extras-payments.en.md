# Extras: paying in parts & transferring the bill

Two things on a booking's **Folio tab**, for extras (restaurant / POS bills, Sales items, activities):

- **Paying in parts**: the guest pays part now and the rest later, for example with two different banks.
- **Transfer bill**: this room's extras are paid by another room, for example two friends who booked separately through an OTA.

## Paying in parts

1. **Folio** tab → **💳 Record Payment**.
2. Tick the extras being paid (usually all are ticked already).
3. In **Extras — pay now**, type the amount received now, e.g. **2.000.000**.
4. Choose the **Method** (e.g. Bank Transfer), add a **Reference** if there is one, and press **Receive**.
5. The window shows what is still to pay. For the rest, open **Record Payment** again. The amount is already filled in with what is left; choose the second bank and press **Receive**.

> A part payment is recorded as a **payment on account**. The balance drops straight away, but the extras lines do **not** get a "Paid" badge. That is normal: look at **Balance due**. Each payment has its own **🖨 receipt** in the payments list.

> **OTA reservations** (Booking.com, Agoda, Traveloka…): the **Room** line in this window starts **unticked**, because the OTA pays the room, not the guest. Tick it only when the OTA's money has arrived in the bank (method = that bank, date = the day it arrived). "Guest owes" at the top is what the guest has to pay.

If you leave the amount as it is (paid in full in one go), everything works as before: the lines get **Paid · method**.

## Transferring the bill to another room

Example: rooms 101 and 102 booked separately on an OTA, but room 101 pays room 102's restaurant bill.

1. Open **room 102's** booking → **Folio** tab.
2. Under **Posted**, press **⇄ Transfer to another room**.
3. Tick the lines to move.
4. Choose the room that pays (**101**). The list shows rooms staying on the same dates.
5. Type a **Reason**, e.g. "Room 101 pays for their friends", and press **Transfer**.

The lines are now on **room 101's** folio, marked **from room 102 · name**, and are paid there (also at room 101's check-out). Room 101's invoice says "(from room 102 …)" too. Both bookings get a note in **Edit History**.

**What can move:** **unpaid** extras, meaning restaurant / POS bills, Sales items and activities.

**What can't move:**
- Room and meal-plan nights. They stay on their own room; an OTA room is paid by the OTA.
- Per-night extras such as an extra bed.
- Lines already paid.
- A stay already on an agent's bill.
- Transfers onto a booking that is checked out or cancelled.

> Moved by mistake? Open the other room's folio and transfer the line back to the room it came from. The mark disappears.
