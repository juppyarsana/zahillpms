# Front desk phone

The **Front Desk Phone** app makes the front desk phone ring like a normal call when a room calls the front desk, also with the screen off or locked. The PC keeps ringing as before; the first to answer gets the call.

## Setting up (once)

1. Install the app on the phone (the APK file from the admin) and open **Front Desk Phone** → **Continue**.
2. On the PMS (owner): **Settings → Front desk phones → Add phone**. A code appears, e.g. `ABCD-2345`, valid 30 minutes.
3. Type the code on the phone → **Pair this phone**.
4. Do everything under **Finish setting up this phone**: microphone, notifications, full-screen calls, battery without restrictions, and Autostart.

**Vivo phones** (needed, or the phone stops ringing after a while):

- Settings → Battery → *Background power consumption* → Front Desk Phone → **Allow**.
- i Manager → **Autostart** → Front Desk Phone **on**.
- The app's notifications: allow all, show on the **lock screen**.
- Open Recents and **lock** the Front Desk Phone card so it isn't cleared.
- Test: lock the phone, wait 30 minutes, then call from a room tablet.

## Using it

- **A room calls:** full screen "Room 104" with **Answer** and **Not now**. *Not now* silences this phone only.
- During the call: **Mute**, **Speaker**, and the red button to hang up. The screen turns off at your ear.
- **Missed calls** and **Call a room** are on the app's main screen, the same as on the PC.
- **Call the restaurant** (when the restaurant line is on) rings the restaurant's POS and tablet. When the restaurant calls, the phone rings with **Restaurant**.
- Back hides the app; the line stays connected (see the "Front desk phone" notification).

## Lost or replaced phone

PMS → **Settings → Front desk phones**: **Remove** (it stops ringing at once), or **Move to another phone** for a new code.
