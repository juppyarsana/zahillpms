# Staff notes on a booking

**Staff notes** are for the team: anything the next shift should know about a booking (transport to arrange, a late check-out agreed, a promise made to the guest). The guest never sees them. They are on the booking page → **Staff Notes** card.

## Write a note

1. Type in the box **Add a note for the team…**. Press Enter for a new line; the note keeps its lines.
2. Optional: **⏰ Remind on a day…** → pick the day the note matters (e.g. the day of the transport).
3. Press **Add note**.

The newest note is on top, with who wrote it and the time.

## Reminders

A note with a reminder day shows **on that day**:

- **Today** → card **Reminders for today**
- **Guest Lists** for that date → card **Reminders for this day**

Click a reminder to open the booking.

## Change or delete a note

The person who wrote the note (or the owner) sees ✏ and 🗑 next to it.

- ✏ **Edit**: change the text or the reminder day → **Save**. The note shows "edited".
- 🗑 **Delete**: asks first.

The old text of an edited or deleted note is kept in **Edit History**.

## Where else notes show

| Where | What shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Check-in / out** — arriving, departing, in-house rows | The latest 2 notes |
| **Check-in / out** — check-in and check-out windows | Up to 5 notes ("anything to settle before the guest leaves?") |
| **Today** — room window | The latest 3 notes |

> **A note with a price is not on the bill.** Transport, a tour or anything the guest pays for must be added on the **Folio** tab → **+ Add item** (or Activities), or it will be forgotten at check-out. The note can say who arranges it.
