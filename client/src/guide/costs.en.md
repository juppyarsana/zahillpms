# Overview & Costs by department

Two pages for the owner and managers. Owners always see them; anyone else needs the **Overview** or **Costs by department** permission (Roles & Permissions).

## Overview

**[Overview](/overview)** is the owner's home: everything at a glance, with what needs you first.

- **Needs you** — complimentary stays and purchase orders waiting for approval, OTA bookings that need someone, guests past check-out, stays that never checked in, agents and supplier bills overdue, and a department whose costs jumped. Tap one to open where it is sorted.
- **Tonight**, **Revenue this month** (compared with the same days of last month), **Money in yesterday**, **Still owed to the hotel**.
- **Biggest costs** this month and the **next 14 nights** booked.

Every figure is the same as on its own page (Reports, Cashier Closing, Receivables, Forecast).

## Costs by department

**[Insights → Costs](/costs)** shows which part of the hotel spends the most, and what changed.

1. Pick the month with ‹ ›. While a month is still running it is compared with the **same days** of the month before.
2. The cards: total costs, the biggest department, the biggest jump, and **cost per occupied room**.
3. **Departments, biggest first** — tap one to see what it spent on, the change on last month and every entry.
4. **Department profit** — what Rooms, Food & Beverage and Activities keep after their own costs, and what each spends for every Rp 100 it brings in.
5. **Last 6 months** — tap a month to open it.

> What is counted: everything entered in **Expenses** and **Payables** (supplier bills), each by its **Department**. Salaries, agent commission and the accountant's journal entries are not in it yet.

A cost in the wrong department? Change the **Department** on the expense or the bill. The supplier's *usual department* (Payables → Suppliers → Edit) fills it in next time.
