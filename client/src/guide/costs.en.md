# Overview & Insights

The owner's and managers' pages: **Overview** at the top of the menu, and the **Insights** group — one question per page:

| Insights | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Every figure on one page, for any period — with Excel / PDF |
| **Sales** → Revenue | How much did we sell, and where did it come from? |
| **Sales** → Rooms | How full were we, which rooms and meal plans sell? |
| **Sales** → Guests | Who are our guests, where are they from, do they come back? |
| **Bookings** → Bookings | How do reservations come in — channels, how far ahead, cancellations? |
| **Bookings** → Forecast | What is on the books for the days ahead? |
| **Money** → Costs | Which department spends the most? |
| **Money** → Profit | What is left after costs? |
| **Export / Print** | Every report to print or download |

Owners always see them; anyone else needs the permission (Roles & Permissions): **Overview**, **Insights — Revenue, Rooms, Guests, Bookings**, **Costs by department**, **Profit**.

Each page compares the period with the one before. While a month is still running, it is compared with the **same days** of the month before.

## Overview

**[Overview](/overview)** is the owner's home: everything at a glance, with what needs you first.

- **Needs you** — complimentary stays and purchase orders waiting for approval, OTA bookings that need someone, guests past check-out, stays that never checked in, agents and supplier bills overdue, and a department whose costs jumped. Tap one to open where it is sorted.
- **Tonight**, **Revenue this month** (compared with the same days of last month), **Money in yesterday**, **Still owed to the hotel**.
- **Biggest costs** this month and the **next 14 nights** booked.

Every figure is the same as on its own page (Reports, Cashier Closing, Receivables, Forecast).

## Costs by department

**[Insights → Costs](/costs)** shows which part of the hotel spends the most, and what changed.

1. Pick the month with ‹ ›. While a month is still running it is compared with the **same days** of the month before.
2. The cards: total costs, the biggest department, the biggest jump, and **cost per occupied room**.
3. **Departments, biggest first** — tap one to see what it spent on, the change on last month and its biggest entries.
   - Tap a **category** to see only its entries, or **See all entries** for the whole department: filter by category, search, sort by biggest or newest.
   - Tap an **entry** to see who recorded it, the supplier, the receipt number and, for a bill, whether it is paid. **Open in Expenses / Payables** takes you to it (if you have that page).
4. **Department profit** — what Rooms, Food & Beverage and Activities keep after their own costs, and what each spends for every Rp 100 it brings in.
5. **Last 6 months** — tap a month to open it.

> What is counted: everything entered in **Expenses** and **Payables** (supplier bills), each by its **Department**. Salaries, agent commission and the accountant's journal entries are not in it yet.

A cost in the wrong department? Change the **Department** on the expense or the bill. The supplier's *usual department* (Payables → Suppliers → Edit) fills it in next time.

## Revenue

**[Insights → Revenue](/revenue)** — what came in and where it came from. The full figures, with Excel and PDF, are on **Insights → Summary**.

- Total revenue, occupancy, average room rate (ADR) and revenue per available room (RevPAR) — and the same month last year when there is one.
- **Every day** — rooms, food & beverage, extras & activities. Hover a day for its figures.
- **Where it came from** — rooms, food & beverage, extras & activities — and links to Bookings, Rooms and Guests for the detail.

All amounts are net — before service charge and tax.

## Profit

**[Insights → Profit](/profit)** — the accountant's Profit & Loss in four steps: revenue → department profit → gross operating profit (GOP) → net profit. Tap a "−" line to see its accounts. The full statement stays in Accounting → Profit & Loss.

> Profit is only as complete as the books. A yellow box says what is missing — for example no journal entries yet for salaries, rent or depreciation. Until those are entered, the profit shown is higher than it really is.

Revenue on Profit is what is posted to the guests' bills, so it can be lower than on the Revenue page while guests have not checked in yet. The page says how much.

## Rooms, Guests and Bookings

These three have **Month / 3 months / Year** at the top, with ‹ › to move, and compare with the period before and the same period last year.

- **[Rooms](/insights/rooms)** — occupancy night by night (month by month for longer periods), average room rate, revenue per available room, every room type, the meal plans sold, how long guests stay.
- **[Guests](/insights/guests)** — the guests who arrived: **where they come from** (countries ranked, tap *See the whole year* for the year's top countries), returning guests, party size, children. Countries go by the nationality of the guest each room is booked under — keep it filled in at check-in.
- **[Bookings](/insights/bookings)** — reservations made, **channels** (OTAs marked), **how far ahead** guests book, cancellations and no-shows, and **pickup**: how many rooms were added for the next 30 nights in the last week.

## Export / Print — every report in one place

**[Insights → Export / Print](/reports)** lists every report you may print or download: guest lists, registration cards, the police guest report, balance due, cashier closing, night audit, the kitchen list, the summary report, costs, forecast, and for the accountant expenses, purchase orders, the journal, Profit & Loss, balance sheet and trial balance.

1. Find the report (or type in **Find a report…**).
2. Tap it, choose the day, month or dates, and press **Download**.

You only see the reports of the pages you can open — nothing new to set up. The buttons on each page still work as before. **Insights → Summary** is the old Reports page: every figure for any period, with Excel / PDF / CSV.
