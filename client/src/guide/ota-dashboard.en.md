# OTA dashboard: slow dates & prices

Open **Sales → OTA dashboard**. It shows which dates need a price decision and the prices we are sending to the OTAs. The red number on the menu is how many slow dates there are in the next 30 nights.

## What a slow date is

A date is **slow** when its occupancy is below its target. The target depends on how close the date is:

| Days before the stay | Slow if below |
|---|---|
| 0–7 | 40% |
| 8–14 | 30% |
| 15–30 | 20% |

These are the starting figures. The owner can change them in **Pricing rules**.

## Next 30 nights

Each night shows its occupancy and, under it, the price we send per room type in Rp million.

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red date | Slow. Press it to change the price |
| Green date | On track |
| Maroon date | 70% full or more |
| Amber price | A manual price typed by someone |

Hover over a price (or tap it on a phone) to see why it is that price and how it differs from the night before. Use the switch at the top right to see another meal plan. These are the prices we send. An OTA's own discounts aren't included, so a guest may see a lower price on the OTA.

## Changing a price

1. Press **Change price** on a slow date, or press the red date in the calendar.
2. Read the left side: where the date stands, how it compares, what else matters, and what we suggest.
3. Press a suggested price to use it, or **Use all suggested**. You can also type your own.
4. If you sell more than one meal plan, switch meal plan and do the same. **Will send** shows how many prices will go.
5. Press **Save and send to OTAs**. It reaches the OTAs within about 20 seconds.

The owner gets a Telegram message, and the change is listed under **Pricing rules → Recent price changes**.

> A price below the room type's minimum can't be saved. Ask the owner if it really needs to go lower.

## Manual prices stay until you remove them

A price you type stays on that date, **even if the date fills up**. The ladder won't raise it. When the date no longer needs it, press **Remove manual price** in the Slow dates list. A date with a manual price stays in that list even once it is on track, so you can find it.

## How the suggestion works

It is a fixed rule, not a prediction:

- At least a quarter below target: 10% lower. Closer to target: 5% lower.
- 3 days or less to go: 5% more.
- More than 14 days to go: 5% less, but never under 5%.
- A room type with fewer than 3 rooms left keeps its price.
- It never goes below the minimum.

"How it compares" only shows the usual booking pace once there are enough past dates to go by. Until then it shows just the bookings of the last 7 days.

## Pricing rules (owner)

**Pricing rules** holds the occupancy targets and the minimum price per room type. Sales can read them, and only the owner can change them. A minimum applies when a price or a ladder is saved. Prices already set are not changed.

## How each OTA is doing

Reservations made in the last 30 days compared with the 30 days before. **Commission** only shows for OTAs that send it through the channel manager; the others read **Not sent**.

## Since yesterday

Bookings that came in or changed since yesterday at 00:00. When an OTA is connected it often sends its old bookings again. Stays already over and bookings already in the PMS don't count as new; how many were left out is written under the list.
