# Moving a guest to another room (Change Room)

Every room move goes through **⋮ More → Change Room** on the reservation. The window asks **From which night?**

## Moving the whole stay

Choose **The whole stay**, pick the new room, then **Change Room**.

- A room of the **same type** (Deluxe → Deluxe): the price stays, a reason is optional.
- A room of **another type** (upgrade / downgrade): the PMS shows the difference. Choose *Charge the difference* or *Complimentary*, and type a reason.
- A guest already checked in: the old room is marked dirty.

## Moving during the stay

Example: the guest stays 20–24 Oct, the first night in **203**, the rest in **101**.

1. **⋮ More → Change Room**.
2. **From which night?** → choose **From 21 Oct**.
3. Pick room **101**. Check the summary:
   - **203** · 20–21 Oct · 1 night · its price
   - **101** (new booking) · 21–24 Oct · 3 nights · its price
   - Money already received: how much **stays on 203** and how much **moves to 101** (same date and method).
4. Type a reason if asked, then press **Split from 21 Oct**.

You get two bookings for the same guest, linked to each other ("The stay continues in room 101 →"). If the guest is part of a group, the new booking joins the same group.

- **Guest not arrived yet:** check in to 203 as usual; on the 21st check out of 203 and check in to 101.
- **Guest in house, moving today:** choose tonight. 203 ends today — check out of 203, then check in to 101.

> Don't shorten the booking and add a new room to move a guest any more. The money stays on the first room (it looks like "to be refunded") and the new room looks unpaid.

## Edit Price and "the received amount was the same typo"

This choice **reduces money already recorded as received**. Use it only when the amount was typed wrong. If the money really came in (e.g. the guest moved rooms), choose *Guest really paid … — keep it*, or use **Change Room → From which night**. The PMS warns you when the money was received on an earlier day.

## Good to know

- If there is an **extra bed** (or another per-night extra) on the nights being moved, remove those nights first, then add them on the new room.
- A complimentary stay, or one already on an agent's bill, can't be split.
- On the group page a room that received more than its price is marked **paid too much** — that is not money to collect again.
