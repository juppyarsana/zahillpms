# Agent credit — money held for a cancelled room

An agent often **pays for their rooms in advance**. When the agent then cancels one room, the hotel still holds that money. It is **not** the hotel's revenue and it is **not lost**: it is **credit held for the agent**. The agent can use it on another stay, or ask for it back.

> The money stays recorded on the cancelled room. The books, Cashier Closing and Daily Close do not change — the PMS only adds up each agent's credit so you can see it and use it.

## 1. Cancel the room as usual

Booking → ⋮ → **Cancel Booking**, with the reason (for example "cancel by agent"). The window says that, for an agent's booking, the money received **becomes credit held for the agent**. Do **not** refund it unless the agent asks.

> The group's total goes down by that room (it is no longer a room to pay). The money does not disappear: it shows as credit.

## 2. See the credit

- **Receivables → the agent's page:** a **Credit held** card at the top — each cancelled room, who it was for, when it was cancelled, how and when it was paid, and the amount.
- **The group page** (when a group room was cancelled) and **any other booking of the same agent** show the same card.

## 3. Use the credit on another stay

1. Open the agent's other booking that still has something to pay (or use the card on the agent's page).
2. On the **Credit held** card press **Use here** (or **Use on a stay** and choose the stay).
3. Type the **amount** — at most the smaller of the credit and what the stay still owes — and a **reason**. You can use only part of the credit.
4. Save. The money is recorded on that stay: its unpaid deposit / balance first, then extras.

The payment keeps its **original date, method and reference**, so no day's money changes. Both bookings' Edit History say what happened.

> Credit can only be used on a stay of **the same agent**, and not on a room of a group paid as one bill (use Record Group Payment there).

## 4. Give the credit back

On the card press **Refund**: amount (up to the credit), **how** the money is given back (bank transfer, cash…), the **date**, the **reference** and a **reason**. It is saved as its own refund line on that day (a minus in Cashier Closing and Daily Close).

## Good to know

- Front desk can use or refund the credit; every use or refund needs a reason and sends an alert to the owner.
- No expiry: the credit stays until it is used or refunded.
- A no-show / late-cancellation fee the hotel keeps is taken off the credit first.
- A payment made for a sale, an activity or a voucher cannot be used as credit — it can only be refunded.
