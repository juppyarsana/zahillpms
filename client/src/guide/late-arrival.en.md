# Guests arriving after midnight (no-show)

The **night audit** runs every night at **03:00**. A guest due in yesterday who hasn't checked in by then is marked **No Show**. A guest who arrives before 03:00 is simply checked in as usual — on **Check-in / out** they're under Arriving, marked *LATE ARRIVAL*.

If the guest arrives **after** being marked no-show:

## One room

1. Open **Check-in / out** (or **Quick Check-in**).
2. In the **🌙 Marked no-show last night** box press **Arrived late — check in**.
3. The check-in opens — carry on as usual, payment included.

Or from the reservation page: the yellow **Marked no-show** box → **↩ Undo No-Show**.

## A group

1. On **Check-in / out**, the **🌙 Marked no-show last night** box → **Arrived late — put all N rooms back**.
   Or on the group page: **More → Guests arrived late — put N rooms back**, or **↩ Undo No-Show** on one room only.
2. The rooms are back under Arriving — press **Check In Whole Group**, then **Record Group Payment**.

> Never press **Cancel Group** for a group that is a no-show — it cancels its rooms. While every room is a no-show the button is hidden.

## Good to know

- The first night is still charged (the room was held for the guest).
- If someone else has the room by now, the PMS refuses and names that guest — move one of the rooms first.
- Once put back, that night's reports (Reports, Daily Close) count the room again.
